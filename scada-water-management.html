Questions about scada & water management

Is this the same as flat-wise water billing?

No. SCADA focuses on plant and distribution operation. Smart water metering records consumption for individual connections or zones.

Can pumps be operated remotely?

Remote operation requires compatible controls, defined permissions and tested local safety interlocks. The proposal specifies the permitted actions.
