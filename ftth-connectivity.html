Questions about ftth & connectivity

Does fibre installation include an internet plan?

Building fibre infrastructure and an internet subscription are separate requirements. Provider arrangements and connectivity charges are defined separately.

Can an existing fibre network be used?

Routes, fibre condition, connector types, splitter losses and equipment compatibility need assessment before reuse.
