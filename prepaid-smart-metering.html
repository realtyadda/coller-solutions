Prepaid smart metering

Give residents visibility of electricity use and help your accounts team manage unit-wise readings, prepaid balances and billing from a connected metering system.

Request a QuoteCall NowWhatsApp ↗

Installation & integration

  • Plan meter quantities, single- or three-phase connections, and mains/DG measurement requirements.
  • Select suitable Elmeasure or HPL metering equipment and assess wired or wireless communication coverage.
  • Configure supported recharge, tariff and consumption-reporting functions, then commission meters and train operators.

What it helps you do

  • Reduce dependence on manual reading rounds through connected meter data.
  • Review consumption and prepaid balances in the selected software.
  • Separate mains and generator consumption where the meter and system support dual-source measurement.

Questions about prepaid smart metering

Can we upgrade an existing installation?

Share the meter make, model, communication type and software details. Compatibility must be checked before deciding whether to retain meters or replace them.

Will residents receive low-balance alerts?

Alerts and recharge options depend on the selected meter, platform and configuration. We define these features in the proposal.
