Questions about parking management & automation

Will RFID work with our existing tags?

Reader frequency, tag format, software compatibility and installation conditions determine whether tags can be reused.

Can vehicle and pedestrian access be separated?

The layout can include separate pedestrian gates where site space and the agreed access design permit.
