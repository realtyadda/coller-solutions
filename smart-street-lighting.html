Questions about smart street lighting

Can every existing LED light be dimmed?

Dimming requires a compatible driver and control interface. Existing fixtures must be checked before specifying it.

How much electricity will it save?

Savings depend on existing operating hours, loads and the revised control strategy. A site baseline is needed before estimating any reduction.
