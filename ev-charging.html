Questions about ev charging solutions

Will every car charge at the charger's rated power?

Actual power depends on the vehicle's charging limit, charger, supply and operating conditions. For AC charging, the vehicle's onboard charger is a key limit.

Can multiple chargers share limited power?

Compatible load-management arrangements may allocate available capacity between chargers. Feasibility depends on the equipment and site design.
