Questions about cctv & surveillance

How many days of footage can be stored?

Retention depends on camera count, resolution, frame rate, compression, recording schedule and usable storage. The proposal should size these together.

Can working cameras be retained?

Existing cameras may be reusable after checking condition, protocols, image quality and compatibility with the proposed recorder.
